Option 1: In-App (Instant)

Directly Inside Android App

If you currently have the app installed on your phone:

  1. Open the Vankar Samaj Matrimony app.
  2. Tap on Profile (પ્રોફાઇલ) tab at the bottom.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of your profile page.
  4. Tap "Delete Account (એકાઉન્ટ કાઢી નાખો)".
  5. Select your reason and confirm.

⚡ Deletion takes effect immediately.

Option 2: Web Form (No App Required)

Submit Online Web Request

If you uninstalled the app or lost your mobile device:

  1. Fill out the verified request form below.
  2. Provide your registered mobile number & email.
  3. State your reason for deletion.
  4. Click "Submit Account Deletion Request".
  5. Receive a confirmation ticket ID.

🕒 Processed within 24 to 72 hours.

⚠️ Important Notice: Permanent & Irreversible Action
Once your account is deleted, your matrimonial profile, biodata, photos, and match interactions will be permanently erased. You will not be able to recover this profile in the future.

Data Category Specific Items Deletion Status
Matrimonial Profile Full Name, Date of Birth, Age, Gender, Marital Status, Samaj, Pargana, Gotra, Native Village, Height, Education, Job Details, Horoscope. Permanently Erased
